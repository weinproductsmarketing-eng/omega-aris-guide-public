# Printing the Omega ARIS owner's booklet

Edition 1.0 · October 2, 2026

- Finished size: 5.5 × 8.5 inches (US half letter).
- 16 pages, including front and back covers; supplied in normal reading order.
- Suggested binding: left-bound saddle stitch, two staples at the fold.
- White margins; no bleed or crop marks. Text uses embedded TrueType fonts. Product images retain their original pixel dimensions; QR codes and callout lines are vector.
- Color artwork is RGB. This is a print-friendly PDF, not a certified PDF/X press file. Let a commercial printer apply its required output profile and imposition.

## Office printer

Open in a PDF viewer that supports booklet printing, such as Adobe Acrobat Reader. Select Print > Booklet, all pages, both sides, left binding, on US Letter paper. Let the Booklet option arrange the pages; do not also use a separate multiple-pages-per-sheet setting. Follow the viewer/printer's duplex instructions and check one folded proof before producing the full run. If manual duplex is needed, print front sides and back sides as directed by the viewer. Fold and staple at the center. Keep at 100% finished-size output when the printer allows it.

## Commercial printer

Supply the reading-order PDF and request a 16-page, 5.5 × 8.5-inch, left-bound saddle-stitched booklet with no bleed. An opaque matte white text stock around 90–120 gsm is a reasonable starting point; confirm fold, paper and package fit with the printer. Ask for a folded proof so text, page sequence, QR scanning and physical package fit can be checked before the production run.
