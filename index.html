YOUR OMEGA SETUP

Position the coin.
Then start your test.

Use a quiet area and a stable surface. Set up one app first, then follow these steps with the operating instructions supplied with your Omega.

  1. Set your phone in the cradle. It sits above the resonator. Keep its microphone uncovered and allow microphone access in the app.
  2. Choose the exact coin. Match gold or silver, the coin’s weight, and its design or year range. Use a suitable scale and calipers to check the actual weight, diameter and thickness against that coin’s reference values.
  3. Position the coin and bumpers. Wein Products’ instructions recommend suitable gloves. Place the coin under the resonator so it contacts the coin’s upper rim as described in those instructions. Adjust the bumpers on either side for a clear audible ring. Avoid rubbing the coin’s surfaces.
  4. Open the app’s test screen. Start its standard listening/recording test. Control names can vary by app and version.
  5. Activate the Omega. For the controls described in Wein Products’ instructions, slide the recessed switch on the right side toward you for ON. The instructions show a pulsing red indicator inside the Omega opening. The left-side manual test button is for a single pulse. If your controls differ, use your unit’s supplied instructions.
  6. Read the result and finish. Let the app finish listening. Read its full result, then compare weight, dimensions and appearance. Turn the Omega and its battery pack OFF when finished.

Positioning and physical controls: Wein Products’ supplied Quick Start and parts diagram. Follow the instructions supplied with your specific unit.

Power, batteries and cables

Wein Products’ instructions describe a rear pack with four alkaline AA batteries and power from a supplied USB cable or AC USB adapter. Check your packing list and follow the pack’s battery markings and your unit’s connection instructions.

If using the pack described in those instructions, its USB switch position enables its USB output; return it to OFF when finished. Charge your phone with its usual charger while setting up the apps.

Confirm the correct cable and port before connecting the Omega, a phone, a scale or calipers. A USB-C-shaped connector alone does not establish compatibility. For connection help, visit WeinProducts.com. Do not recharge alkaline AA batteries.

If the sound or result is unclear

Recheck the exact coin selection, microphone permission, background noise and coin-to-resonator contact. Adjust the bumpers using the supplied handling instructions. Check the resonator for wear and follow your unit’s replacement guidance.

Read the result for the selected coin rather than aiming for one universal peak count. A sound match is a screening result, not an authenticity guarantee. An unclear or non-matching result calls for more investigation.

What about the ValuePAK?

Check the packing list supplied with your order for the battery pack, spare resonator, batteries, cables and adapter included with your unit. Customers download and pay for their own software. For package or cable questions, contact Wein Products.

ADD A SECOND CHECK

Then try the other app.

Finish one app’s test before switching to the other, and select the same exact coin in each. You can use the apps on one compatible device, or repeat the test on a second compatible device—for example, StackerTools on an iPhone and Pingcoin on an Android phone. Compare both results with the coin’s weight, dimensions and appearance. Agreement between two sound tests is not proof of authenticity.

OPTIONAL · FOR YOUR RECORDS

Keep the result with the coin’s details.

For a collection, shop or institution, a simple record can help you compare tests later. Save a screenshot of the completed result using your device’s screenshot feature.

  1. Give the coin an item or inventory ID and note the date.
  2. Record the selected coin type, app, actual weight, diameter and thickness.
  3. Save the result screenshot with those notes. If you repeat with the other app, keep both results under the same item ID.

These are records you save on your device. Do not assume the Omega or either app automatically creates an audit log or proves chain of custody.

How do I take a screenshot?

iPhone with Face ID: briefly press the side button and Volume Up together. iPhone models with a Home button use a different combination; see Apple’s screenshot guide.

Android: commonly, press Power and Volume Down together. If that does not work, use your manufacturer’s instructions or Google’s screenshot guide.

For an iPad or computer, use the screenshot method provided by that device’s maker.

A LITTLE HELP

Something not working?

I can’t find or install the app.

Use the official store links in this guide. Check your Internet connection, available storage and the operating-system requirement. If your device is marked incompatible, use another supported device. Avoid unofficial download files.

The app cannot hear the coin.

Check the app’s microphone permission in your device settings. Keep the phone microphone clear and reduce background noise. Close another app that may be using the microphone. Exact settings labels depend on your device. Choose your device above for the illustrated microphone step.

StackerTools is asking for a password or code.

A free download does not guarantee full access. The developer says the Pocket Pinger Bonus Pack contains an access code. Omega ownership alone does not establish that entitlement. Ask through the developer’s official site how to obtain access; do not use a shared code from an unofficial source.

I paid on another device.

Use the store account that made the purchase. Check the app for a purchase-restoration option if provided. Do not assume an Android purchase transfers to iPhone, or that one StackerTools code covers unlimited devices; check the developer’s purchase terms.

The test fails or the two apps disagree.

Check the selected coin, size, type and year range. Reduce noise and repeat only with the handling instructions supplied with your Omega. A non-match is a reason to investigate, not a final counterfeit diagnosis. Compare weight, dimensions and visual details before deciding what to do.

Which cable or battery connection do I use?

Follow the instructions supplied with your unit. If you are unsure, contact Wein Products with your unit and cable details before connecting them. Do not recharge alkaline AA batteries.
