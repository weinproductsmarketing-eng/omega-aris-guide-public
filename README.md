## A little guidance. A confident start.

This guide helps Omega ARIS owners get **Pingcoin** or **StackerTools** ready for coin testing. Choose your device, follow the steps for one app, and complete a simple readiness check.

Designed for bullion collectors, shops and institutions, with readable text, large controls and a layout that adapts to phones, tablets and computers.

### Choose your route

| Your device | Where to begin |
| --- | --- |
| Android phone or tablet | Open the app’s Google Play listing on your device and check compatibility. |
| iPhone or iPad | Open the app’s App Store listing on your device and check compatibility. |
| Windows, Mac or Linux computer | Use the developer’s browser tester with Internet and microphone access. |

Customers obtain and pay for their own software. Prices, access terms and supported devices may change; check the developer’s current offer before purchasing. StackerTools identifies its browser tester as beta.

> A sound test is one part of checking a coin. Compare weight, dimensions and appearance as well. Agreement between apps is not proof of authenticity.

## Open the website

The website is ready for **GitHub Pages**. Once Pages is enabled, GitHub displays the shareable address under **Settings → Pages** and in the repository’s **Deployments** area.

To publish this repository:

1. Open **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select **main** and **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
4. Wait for the deployment to finish and open the address GitHub provides.

You can also download the repository, extract it, and open `index.html` in your browser. Keep the files and `assets` folder together.

## Built to stay simple

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. **No installation, account, API key or build step is needed to view the guide.** The third-party testing services have their own requirements.

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | Page structure, device choices, readiness checks and help |
| `styles.css` + `modern.css` | Responsive layout, cinematic introduction and readable cards |
| `motion.js` | Scroll-linked video and motion preferences |
| `app.js` | Guide selection and product gallery |
| `content.js` | App-specific English instructions |
| `assets/` | Product views and app screen examples |
| `sources.json` | Public reference links |

The guide is currently in **U.S. English**. App-specific text is separated from rendering to make future reviewed translations easier.
